Concept projectGenerative campaign

Alba

Key visual · 4:5

Brief

Launch a fictional organic tea brand with a campaign that feels hand-crafted, produced end to end with generative tools.

Process

  1. Moodboard and art direction: warm sunrise palette, soft grain and tactile ceramics.
  2. Style frames in Midjourney with a fixed style reference so every visual matches.
  3. Vertical videos animated from the key frames in Kling and Runway.
  4. Voiceover and sound design with ElevenLabs.
  5. Retouch, typography and final edit in Photoshop, After Effects and Premiere.

Tools

  • Midjourney
  • Kling
  • Runway
  • ElevenLabs
  • Photoshop
  • After Effects
  • Premiere

Deliverables

  • 6 key visuals (4:5 and 16:9)
  • 3 vertical videos (9:16, 15 s)
  • Prompt and style guide for the brand team

Concept project: a self-initiated brief that shows how I work. It is not a client commission.

Concept projectAI agent workflow

Claude content agent

Brief

Remove the repetitive steps of a weekly social calendar without losing art direction.

Process

  1. The brief lands in a Notion database.
  2. A Claude agent with custom Skills (brand voice, visual rules) drafts copy and image prompts.
  3. MCP connectors send the prompts to the image tools and place the results in Canva templates.
  4. I review, adjust and approve every piece.
  5. Approved posts go to the scheduling queue.

Tools

  • Claude
  • Claude Code
  • Claude Skills
  • MCP
  • Notion
  • Canva

Deliverables

  • Agent workflow and custom skills
  • Brand voice and visual rules as reusable skills
  • Human review checklist

Concept project: a self-initiated brief that shows how I work. It is not a client commission.

Concept projectConsistent brand character

Nina

Turnaround

Brief

Design a character for a fictional fintech app and keep it consistent across AI-generated scenes.

Process

  1. Character design and turnaround sheet drawn by hand.
  2. Reference set and prompt rules that lock proportions, colors and outfit.
  3. Scene generation with image references, discarding off-model results.
  4. Paint-over and cleanup for final quality.
  5. Short animated loops for the app onboarding.

Tools

  • Illustrator
  • Photoshop
  • Midjourney
  • Runway
  • After Effects

Deliverables

  • Turnaround and expression sheet
  • 10 scene illustrations
  • 3 onboarding loops
