How I work with AI

AI speeds up production. Art direction keeps it on-brand. Every piece goes through the same four steps.

  1. 01

    Brief & direction

    Goal, audience and visual references. I define the look before generating a single frame.

  2. 02

    Style frames & prompts

    Key frames and prompt systems that lock in style, characters and brand colors.

  3. 03

    Generation & curation

    Image, video and voice with tools like Midjourney, Runway, Kling, Veo and ElevenLabs. I keep only what works.

  4. 04

    Compositing & final edit

    Design, compositing, motion and sound in After Effects and Premiere for a finished, human result.
