Raíz

Raíz
Tostadores · Café
AaFraunces

Brief

Build an identity that feels artisanal but works on packaging, social media and a storefront.

Process

  1. Research and positioning against other local roasters.
  2. Italic serif wordmark with an accent that echoes a coffee seed.
  3. Earth-tone palette and a packaging label system by origin.
  4. Social media templates for launches and origin stories.

Tools

  • Illustrator
  • Photoshop
  • Figma

Deliverables

  • Logo and variations
  • Color palette and typography
  • Packaging label system
  • Social media kit

Concept project: a self-initiated brief that shows how I work. It is not a client commission.

Pulso

pulso
Hábitos que se sienten
AaBricolage Grotesque

Brief

Give a wellbeing app a brand that feels motivating without the clichés of fitness apps.

Process

  1. Brand attributes workshop: energetic, kind, honest.
  2. Pulse-dot symbol and a rounded grotesk wordmark.
  3. Gradient system translated into UI color tokens.
  4. Launch campaign visuals for social and app stores.

Tools

  • Figma
  • Illustrator
  • After Effects

Deliverables

  • Logo and app icon
  • Color and gradient tokens
  • Typography system
  • Launch social kit

Concept project: a self-initiated brief that shows how I work. It is not a client commission.
